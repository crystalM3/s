Privacy policy

Online version · Draft updated November 8, 2026

This is a preview of the privacy policy for the online version in development. It outlines the intended data use for shared temple rooms and interactions. Service providers, retention periods, and the final data scope will be confirmed before launch.

1. Scope and contact

This draft covers the planned online version of Quiet Temple: Wooden Fish (靜心山寺：木魚與念珠) and this information website. Privacy questions may be directed to the developer contact email on the official Google Play listing. The final publisher identity and direct contact address will be included in the launch policy.

2. Data needed for shared interactions

The online design calls for a visitor or session identifier, the room you join, your character’s position and facing direction, bowing and seat states, and incense queue participation. These data are intended to coordinate shared rooms and display interactions to other visitors in the same room. The identifier and reconnection design are still being finalized; this draft does not assume that a registered account is required.

3. Connection and service security

Operating a network service may require processing IP addresses, connection times, app versions, error events, and limited security logs to establish connections, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse. The final policy will specify which fields are collected and the applicable purposes and legal bases. Ritual actions will not be used to infer a visitor’s religious beliefs.

4. Preferences and progress

Audio preferences and bead counts support the game experience. The online version’s storage and synchronization design is not yet final. Before launch, the policy will distinguish information kept on the device from information sent to a service, including any recovery or cloud-save functionality.

5. Visibility and service providers

Shared-room interactions are intended to be visible to other visitors in that room. Network and hosting providers may process the technical information needed to operate the service. We will identify the actual providers, processing regions, international transfer arrangements where applicable, and any additional SDKs before release. This draft does not authorize the sale of personal information or advertising use of ritual activity.

6. Retention and your choices

Room presence and action states are intended for session coordination. The retention and cleanup periods for session identifiers, progress, and service logs must be specified in the final policy. You may clear device-stored preferences through Android settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not by itself delete information held by an online service; the launch policy will explain how to request access, correction, or deletion and exercise other applicable privacy rights.

7. This website

This information website does not set cookies, use browser local storage, run analytics, or load tracking scripts. Language selection is carried in the page URL. When hosted, the provider may process IP addresses, browser information, request times, and requested paths in access or security logs. Hosting and log retention settings must be confirmed before publication.

8. Children and sensitive information

The planned temple interactions do not require contacts, photographs, precise location, camera or microphone access, or a declaration of religious affiliation. The online service’s age eligibility and any parental consent requirements will be confirmed before launch. Parents and guardians may contact the developer with privacy concerns.

9. Launch policy and updates

This preview is not a statement that the online service is already operating. A finalized policy with an effective date, confirmed data practices, and contact details will be published before the online version launches, together with matching Google Play data-safety and in-app disclosures.
